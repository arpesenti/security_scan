Look for:
- Use of weak or broken algorithms (MD5, SHA1, DES, RC4, ECB mode, unkeyed hashing)
- Hardcoded cryptographic keys, IVs, salts, or passwords in source code
- Missing encryption for sensitive data at rest or in transit

- TLS/SSL configuration issues (weak ciphers, expired certificates, self-signed certs used in prod)
- Information leakage through cryptographic operations (timing attacks, side channels)
- Use of deprecated or removed cryptographic standards
- Missing integrity verification for encrypted data (no MAC, no HMAC, no AEAD)
